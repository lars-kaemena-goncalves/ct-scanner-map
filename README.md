# ct-scanner-map
Find out whether an airport uses CT scanners at security, so you know if laptops and liquids can stay in your bag. Data is scraped from official sources and verified by the community.

It was created as a small personal side project. The idea came suddenly as I was transferring through the third different airport on a day and wondered whether the airport had new scanners installed in their security lines, therefore alleviating me from taking out all technical belongings and allowing me to take any liquid larger than 100ml with me.

After some quick research, I found out that there is no holistic website showcasing or compiling data across airports, and especially if you are transferring through multiple airports regularly, you do not want to find yourself checking with every single airport FAQ page all the time. So, the idea for this project was born.

I hope that you enjoy this uni student's side project and find it helpful for yourself.

Safe travels and happy landings!
